Privacy & disclosures
✕
Your trades and dividends live in your own database — SQLite on disk or your own Postgres. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no analytics or telemetry in this app.
Market data comes from public endpoints only: TWSE MIS for Taiwan quotes, Yahoo for US quotes and daily history, FinMind for Taiwan monthly revenue. No broker login is ever required.
The assistant is opt-in. When you ask a question, your portfolio snapshot is sent to the provider you chose, using your own API key. The key is stored in the iOS Keychain and never on the server.
This app reports your own records and public data. It does not provide investment advice or recommendations, and prices may be delayed.
